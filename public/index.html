selfsender

Send a Slack DM to a list of recipients, as the bot or as your own (user-token) account.

No tokens configured yet? Install to Slack to get SLACK_BOT_TOKEN/SLACK_USER_TOKEN written to .env automatically (then restart the server).

Logged in via Hack Club Auth. Log out

Comma-separated. Extra spaces around each entry are ignored.
Lets you tell messages from different sends apart at a glance. Requires the bot token to have the chat:write.customize scope.
Warning: this site only sends messages — it cannot show you replies. If a recipient responds, that reply lands in the bot's own DM with them inside Slack (visible to whoever has access to the bot/app there), not here. Check the bot's messages directly in Slack to see or answer replies.
